## Groomed Product Backlog for the University Outreach Website

This groomed product backlog translates the **full published Assignment 2 scope**—the fixed outcomes for **Sprint 1, Sprint 2, and Sprint 3**—into traceable user stories (**US-01** through **US-10**) with priorities, acceptance criteria, dependencies, and **story-point estimates for every sprint’s planned work**, not only for the first sprint. In the controlled Scrum context of SWEN90016, grooming is done so that each item is defined clearly enough for **Sprint Planning and Sprint Backlog preparation when that sprint is planned**, while **not** expanding scope beyond the published outcomes or reprioritising the sprint sequencing set by the teaching team.

## Definition of Done (DoD)

A user story is considered **Done** when all of the following are true:

- The implemented or configured behaviour satisfies the agreed acceptance criteria for that story.
- The story has been checked against the published sprint outcome and does not exceed the approved scope.
- Required content, configuration, or data entries for the story have been prepared and verified at the level needed for demonstration.
- A teammate has reviewed the story outcome for correctness, clarity, and alignment with the sprint objective.
- No unresolved issue remains that would prevent the story from being demonstrated honestly in Sprint Review.
- Relevant Scrum artefacts in Git have been updated so the scope, status, and assumptions remain traceable.
- The story has been implemented or configured using the approved WordPress environment and allowed tooling for the assignment.
- Acceptance criteria have been verified through structured team 
  review using BDD-style test scenarios (Given-When-Then), and at 
  least one peer team member who did not implement the story has 
  confirmed the outcome before the story is marked Done.
- Any defects identified during acceptance review have been recorded 
  in the Defect Log and either resolved or formally accepted before 
  the story is marked Done.
