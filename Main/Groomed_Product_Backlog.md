## Definition of Done (DoD)

A user story is considered Done when all of the following are true:

- The implemented or configured behaviour satisfies the acceptance criteria for that story.
- Acceptance criteria have been checked through QA or peer review and recorded in the sprint artefacts.
- Required content, configuration, or data entries have been prepared and validated at the level needed for demonstration.
- Any issue or defect discovered during delivery has been updated in the relevant tracker, or the team has recorded that no applicable defect entry is required.
- Demo evidence is prepared so the story can be shown honestly rather than described only in words.
- Relevant Git artefacts are updated so scope, status, assumptions, and validation state remain traceable.
- The story is implemented or configured using the approved WordPress environment and allowed tooling for the assignment.
